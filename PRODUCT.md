# AVA and MemorX investor presentations

This existing site lets investors explore two separate product presentations without an account. AVA covers an AI companion shaped by shared conversations with both participants’ consent. MemorX covers photographs reconstructed into explorable spaces with memories kept in context.

## Current extension

The root URL presents two equally prominent choices, AVA and MemorX. Selecting either opens its deck on slide 1. Existing product and slide links remain available. The brand link and presentation settings provide a return to the choices.

The chooser uses the existing product logos, colours and typography. It supports desktop, mobile and keyboard navigation. Video and 3D payloads load only after entering a presentation. Invalid or absent product queries show the chooser.

## Scope

This extension changes entry and return navigation. It does not alter the products’ claims, funding terms or presentation content. It has no account, form, messaging or investor follow-up workflow.

The shipped presentations remain the authority for product and financing content. The chooser direction is recorded in `.impeccable/surfaces/product-selector.md`.
